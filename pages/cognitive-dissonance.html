Cognitive Dissonance

Summary

Cognitive dissonance occurs when a person holds two or more contradictory beliefs, ideas, or values, or performs an action that contradicts their beliefs. This tension motivates people to resolve the inconsistency by changing their attitudes, beliefs, or behaviors.

Core Elements

  • Inconsistency: Discrepancy between actions and beliefs.
  • Psychological Tension: Discomfort that triggers coping mechanisms.
  • Resolution Strategies: Changing behavior, justifying behavior, or rationalizing.

💡 Real-Life Analogy

Imagine someone who knows smoking is bad for health but smokes anyway. To reduce discomfort, they might tell themselves: "It helps me relax, so the benefit outweighs the risk."

Key Terms

Term Definition Example
Attitude Change Modifying beliefs to align with behavior. Believing a task was fun after being paid $1.
Rationalization Creating logical reasons to justify behavior. "I'll exercise twice as hard tomorrow."
